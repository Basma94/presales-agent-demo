Company Brief
SCIT Group is a Saudi-based technology investment fund established May 2022, founded by Sheikh Saad Al-Otaibi. Focused on space, end-to-end security and aviation through advanced communications and emerging technologies, with a strategic focus on the Middle East, Africa and Turkey — explicitly aligned with Saudi Vision 2030. SCIT owns/backs a ~300-site private mobile network delivering Europe Inflight Connectivity, currently served by T-Mobile, and its IFC subsidiary SKYFive Arabia is already expanding A2G connectivity into South Africa.
